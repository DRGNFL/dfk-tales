Arena

Squads come from the Legion Viewer: open it, press Arena squad, and put three heroes a side with their pets and gear. Here you choose each hero's Codex, one skill per tier, then fight. Knight, Archer and Priest have their skills; the other classes fight with basic attack, traits and pet for now.

Battle log

    Version 0.2. Gear and pets are the real ones from the chain; a hero without a weapon or armour fights with the Squire weapon of its class and Medium armour, on loan. Melee or ranged comes from the weapon: melee reaches the first standing enemies, ranged reaches anyone.

    Skills from the patch notes up to 2.8.2, traits from the class pages, pet bonuses from the game's list. Stats, accuracy, Speed and starting Initiative: Hubert's Dynamic Stat Calculator. Each battle has a seed: the same seed and the same moves give the same battle.
