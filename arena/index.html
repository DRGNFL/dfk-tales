    Version 0.7. Choose the hero of each place with Change (it opens the Legion Viewer on that place); the computer drafts three heroes near your level from the legions kept in this browser, with random skills, and plays them. Heroes fight with what they wear: the chain's gear, or what you put on them from the icons around the portrait of their card. Without a weapon or armour a hero fights with the Squire weapon of its class and Medium armour, on loan.

    Two players: Challenge a friend gives a link; your friend opens it, chooses a squad at your level cap and accepts, and you fight live, 40 seconds a move (when the time runs out the hero attacks). The two browsers talk directly (WebRTC, through PeerJS's free meeting point): both compute the same battle from the same seed, only the moves travel, and every move is checked by both. Nothing is staked and nothing is signed.

    Skills from the patch notes up to 2.8.2 and, where they leave a gap, the game client's own texts; icons, scenes and visual effects from the game. Traits from the class pages, pet bonuses from the game's list. Stats, accuracy, Speed and starting Initiative: Hubert's Dynamic Stat Calculator. Each battle has a seed: the same seed and the same moves give the same battle.
