    Version 0.4. Gear and pets are what each hero wears in this browser, set from its card; without a weapon or armour a hero fights with the Squire weapon of its class and Medium armour, on loan. Melee or ranged comes from the weapon.

    Skills from the patch notes up to 2.8.2 and, where they leave a gap, the game client's own texts; icons from the game client. Traits from the class pages, pet bonuses from the game's list. The computer opponent tries each legal move a few times and plays the one that does most. Stats, accuracy, Speed and starting Initiative: Hubert's Dynamic Stat Calculator. Each battle has a seed: the same seed and the same moves give the same battle.
